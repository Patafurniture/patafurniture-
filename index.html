The Process

How PataFurniture Works

A simple, stress-free journey from your vision to your dream home.

1

Share Your Vision

Reach out via WhatsApp, call, or our contact form. Tell us about your space, style preferences, budget range, and timeline. Photos of your space are a huge help!

2

We Do the Research

Our team explores the market — visiting suppliers, comparing options, and curating a selection of pieces that match your brief. We present you with options, photos, and pricing.

3

You Approve, We Negotiate

Once you pick your favourites, we go to work on getting you the best deal. You'll always know the exact cost before any purchase is made.

4

Purchase & Quality Check

We handle the purchase, inspect everything for quality, and prepare for transport. No surprises — just peace of mind.

5

Delivery & Installation

Your furniture arrives safely at your doorstep. Our team assembles and positions everything according to your preferences. You sit back, relax, and enjoy your new space!
